About our leather

Full-grain, vegetable-tanned, Italian

Every KASS journal is made from full-grain Italian vegetable-tanned leather. Full-grain leather keeps the natural surface of the hide, so each piece shows its own grain and markings, and the colour deepens into a rich patina with time and use.

Textured leathers balance flexibility and structure. Pull-up leathers are rich in oils and waxes that lighten as the leather bends, for a naturally lived-in look. Smooth leathers have a clean surface and a more structured feel.

Tap any colour to see its surface, inside and a finished journal, then choose it in Design Your Journal.
