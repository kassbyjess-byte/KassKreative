Which size is right for you?

A5 · fits 148 × 210 mm notebooks
Our classic size, with room to write, plan and sketch. Comes with 3 notebooks and a KASS dust bag. $158 CAD, or from $208 CAD as a custom journal.

A6 · fits 105 × 148 mm notebooks
Compact and easy to carry every day. Comes with 2 notebooks and a KASS dust bag. $128 CAD, or from $178 CAD as a custom journal.

Refillable by design
The elastic binding holds your notebooks in place, so you can swap in new ones when the pages are full and keep the cover you love.

Standard or custom
Standard journals let you choose the leather, elastic, eyelets and embossed initials. Custom journals add an embroidered or hand-stippled initial, a laced spine or the wave design.
