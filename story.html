A little care.
A long story

From your first design to the patina that comes with time.

How do I order?

Choose your journal and details, then send your request through the designer. We’ll confirm availability, your design, and the total by email before payment.

How do I pay?

Once your design and total are agreed, we’ll send a secure Stripe payment link. Making begins after payment.

How long will my journal take?

Please allow up to 14 business days for your journal to be made after payment. Delivery time is additional.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Shipping is quoted for your address before payment. Any import duties or taxes are the customer’s responsibility.

Can I return or cancel my order?

All paid orders are final sale and cannot be returned, exchanged, or cancelled. Please check your design carefully before payment. Sending a request does not commit you to an order.

Will the leather look exactly like the photos?

Each hide has natural variations in grain, colour, and markings. Photos are a guide, and colours may appear differently on your screen.

What if a colour is unavailable?

You can request any colour in the collection. We’ll check availability before payment and discuss alternatives if needed.

How do I care for my leather?

Keep it dry and away from direct sunlight or heat. If wet, let it air-dry naturally. Use a suitable leather conditioner sparingly, testing a hidden area first.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

Who we are

KASS Kreative is a small leather goods studio in Gatineau, Quebec. Jessica, the owner, is responsible for protecting the personal information we hold. You can reach her at kassbyjess@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send a design request, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you add one), shipping address, your design choices and any inspiration photo you attach. When you join our email list, we collect your email address. Our website also uses Google Analytics, which uses cookies to tell us how the site is used, such as the pages visited, the type of device and an approximate location.

How we use it

We use your request details to reply to you, confirm your design and total, send your secure payment link, make your journal and ship it. We only send newsletter emails if you signed up for them. Analytics help us understand which pages are useful so we can improve the site.

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with the services that help us run the studio: Cloudflare (website and secure storage), Resend (email notifications), Stripe (payments), Canada Post (shipping) and Google Analytics. Some of these services store information outside Quebec and Canada, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

Design requests that don’t become orders are deleted automatically after about 13 months. Order and payment records are kept as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing kassbyjess@gmail.com. Every newsletter email includes a way to unsubscribe. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s opt-out tool for Google Analytics.
