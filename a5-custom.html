A5 custom | leather journal

From $208 CAD

Description

A little more space to write, plan, and collect your thoughts. A refillable leather cover for the pages you fill and the ones still to come.

Size & what’s included

Fits A5 notebooks (148 × 210 mm).

Includes: 3 notebooks + KASS dust bag.

Leather & craftsmanship

Handmade in Canada from full-grain Italian vegetable-tanned leather. Natural variations make each cover unique, and the leather develops a rich patina with time and use.

Leather colours

Choose from 15 full-grain Italian vegetable-tanned leathers in three finishes:

Textured: Anthracite, Burgundy, Emerald, Rust, Sky and Tiffany.

Pull-up: Charcoal, Coffee and Olive.

Smooth: Aubergine, Chocolate, Cognac, Forest, Onyx and Scarlett.

See every leather up close

Refillable by design

Fill the pages, replace the notebooks, keep the cover. The elastic binding holds your notebooks in place and lets you swap them when you’re ready.

Custom details

Choose hand-stitched or hand-stippled initials, a lace spine, or the wave journal. One customization is included. Each additional customization is $40 CAD. Have another idea? Tell us your vision for a personalised quote.

Shipping & returns

Shipping is quoted for your address before payment. International shipping is available; any import duties or taxes are the customer’s responsibility.

All paid orders are final sale and cannot be returned, exchanged, or cancelled. Sending a request does not commit you to an order.

Availability, final pricing, and shipping are confirmed by email before payment.
